Welcome to Scan Excess App!

This quick guide will help you get started:

  • Data Entry Form (Left Side):
    • Fill in details like Barcode, Quantity, Box #, Pallet #.
    • Remarks: Choose 'Excess' or 'Stocks Transfer'. Note the validation for 'Stocks Transfer' (Box # starts with SVI-B, Pallet # with SVI-P).
    • Lock Remarks: Check the 'Lock' box next to Remarks to keep the same selection for multiple scans.
    • Quick Scan Mode: Use the 'Quick Scan Mode' button to hide Date, Time, and Remarks for faster entry.
    • Barcode Focus: The Barcode field automatically refocuses after each entry for continuous scanning.
  • Data Table (Right Side):
    • View, Edit, Delete, Sort, and Filter your entries.
    • Search & Filters: Use the search bar for Barcode/Box # and the date pickers for date range filtering.
    • Bulk Delete: Delete all entries for a specific Pallet #.
  • Dashboard: Click the 'Dashboard' button for a quick overview of your scanning activity and trends.
  • Export: Use the 'Export to CSV' button to download your data, either all or filtered by Pallet #.
  • Storage Indicator: Keep an eye on the storage bar in the top right. Export and clear data if it gets too high!

Happy Scanning!
